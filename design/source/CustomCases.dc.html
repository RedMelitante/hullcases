Custom FAQ

Custom order questions

What can you customize?
Foam interiors (custom-cut or bubble foam), custom panels and case printing or logos. If you need something else, choose “Other” in the form and describe it.
How much does a custom case cost?
Every custom job is quoted on its own, based on the case model, the work involved and the quantity. Send the form and we’ll reply with a quote.
Do I need to send you my equipment?
Measurements and photos are enough for a quote. We only guarantee a perfect fit when you send the physical parts to our facility to be fitted into the custom foam.
Can I return a custom case?
No. Custom cases, modified or printed cases, and cases with glued-in foam are made to order and final sale, so please check every detail before you approve.
