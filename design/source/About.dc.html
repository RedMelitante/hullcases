Our story

Rugged protection you can trust

For more than a decade, we've supplied high-performance protective cases built to withstand extreme conditions, whether for industry, fieldwork, travel or everyday use.

From tactical-grade safety boxes to everyday storage, our cases are made for reliability, strength and long-term protection: polypropylene alloy shells, IP67 sealing and a lifetime warranty.
