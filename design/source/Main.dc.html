How do I pick the right size?
Measure your gear (length × width × height), then compare it with the internal size on the product page. Each model lists its internal size, overall dimensions and lid and base depths.
Are the cases waterproof?
Yes. Hull cases are rated IP67: dust-tight and protected against temporary immersion in water.
Can I take a Hull case on a plane?
Our cases are suitable for airline check-in, and many sizes work as carry-on. Check your airline's size and weight limits before you travel.
Do you offer custom foam or logos?
Yes. We offer custom foam lining, custom panels and custom logos. Request a custom case quote.
