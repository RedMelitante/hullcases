Hull Cases · Design system v1

Road-case industrial

Graphite and brushed-steel neutrals, one safety-orange accent, condensed all-caps headings and a corner-bracket motif borrowed from case hardware. Every token below maps to a Tailwind theme key, and the same tokens restyle Medusa's cart, checkout and account pages.
